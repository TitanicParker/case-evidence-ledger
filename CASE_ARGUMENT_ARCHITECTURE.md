# Case Evidence Ledger — Case Argument Architecture

**Status:** Controlled argument-architecture layer  
**Primary basis:** `PROPOSITION_REGISTER.md`  
**Supporting basis:** `EVIDENTIAL_TENSIONS.md`, `FACT_REGISTER.md`, `sources-consolidated-evidence-corpus.md`

> **Control rule:** This document arranges controlled propositions into an argumentative structure. It does not add new historical facts and does not determine negligence, breach of duty, exclusive medical causation, dishonesty, motive or legal liability.

---

# 1. Governing documentary case

The strongest documentary case is not that every historical foot symptom was neurological, nor that Podiatry was inappropriate, nor that levodopa necessarily had to be commenced in July 2017.

It is narrower:

> **The contemporaneous documentary record begins with one evolving unresolved presentation. During the July 2017 admission the hospital identified severe Parkinsonism, genuine structural foot pathology and dystonic-looking lower-limb features, but expressly left the relationship between those domains “Not clear”. A definite operational architecture nevertheless emerged: Parkinsonism remained within Neurology, the feet were routed through Podiatry, dopaminergic treatment was deliberately deferred and Parkinson's became an operative administrative category. Subsequent pharmacological, neurological and genetic evidence repeatedly became capable of bearing on the original unresolved relationship. The surviving record documents local treatment and interpretive updating more clearly than an equivalent reconstruction of the original case architecture.**

The architecture below tests that case in six stages.

---

# 2. Stage One — What entered Neurology?

## Question

Was the hospital presented with two already-separated problems — Parkinsonism and unrelated feet — or with one evolving presentation whose internal relationships were still unresolved?

## Controlling propositions

- `P001` — Integrated neurological entry state
- `P002` — Structural foot pathology and neurological abnormality coexisted
- `P003` — July 2017 left the foot–neurology relationship unresolved

## Argument

The pre-admission GP record is the cleanest control source because it records the presentation before the hospital's later classification became the dominant organising frame.

The GP first documented bilateral foot pain and abnormal gait. The record then evolved: impaired initiation of movement; explicit consideration of Neurology for the patient's “gait, feet and 'muscle tension' issues”; Occupational Health concern; marked increased tone in both legs; worsening dexterity; inability to perform work; and direct neurological assessment.

That chronology does not prove one mechanism. It does establish that the patient arrived at hospital through a **single evolving neurological referral pathway** in which feet, gait, tone, movement initiation, dexterity and function were materially connected enough to require neurological investigation.

The July hospital record then preserves both sides of the presentation: genuine structural foot abnormalities and severe neurological findings, including a footdrop-like gait somewhat suggestive of dystonic posture. Crucially, the relationship is not recorded as resolved. It is recorded as **“Not clear”.**

## Strongest alternative explanation

The structural foot disease was independently real and could appropriately have been managed by Podiatry even while Neurology addressed Parkinsonism.

## Surviving point after the alternative explanation

That explanation supports parallel care, but it does not convert the unresolved relationship into a documented finding of neurological separateness.

## Evidential status

**Document-only.** No later patient recollection is required.

---

# 3. Stage Two — What happened to the unresolved presentation?

## Question

How did an expressly unresolved relationship become a definite operational arrangement?

## Controlling propositions

- `P004` — Operational allocation preceded documentary resolution
- `P005` — Podiatry allocation cannot itself prove neurological separateness
- `P006` — Syndrome certainty and etiological certainty were different
- `P007` — Administrative Parkinson's classification became operative while aetiology remained open
- `P013` — Epistemic limitation coexisted with durable operative authority

## Argument

The record shows a transition from **uncertainty at the level of explanation** to **definiteness at the level of management and administration**.

Neurology retained the Parkinsonian syndrome. Podiatry became the practical pathway for the feet. Parkinson's disease became an administrative category. A deliberate treatment-deferral policy was established.

The important documentary point is not that these actions were necessarily wrong. It is that they became operative **before the internal relationship within the original presentation had been resolved**.

The 6 July GP note sharpens this point. Podiatry was being asked to prioritise the case “as Parkinson's”. Therefore, the mere existence of a Podiatry pathway cannot later be treated as proof that the feet had already been clinically demonstrated to be unrelated to the neurological syndrome.

At the same time, the hospital record distinguished syndrome-level certainty from etiological certainty. Parkinsonism was sufficiently established for treatment and administration while subtype, genetics and the foot relationship remained open.

The record also expressly contains the treating neurologist's statement that he was not a movement-disorders neurologist. That does not establish lack of competence or authority. It does identify a documentary asymmetry: **claimed interpretive limitation coexisted with durable operative decision-making**.

## Strongest alternative explanation

Clinical systems routinely make practical allocations before every causal question is resolved. Administrative coding can operate at syndrome level, and Podiatry can appropriately manage visible structural disease.

## Surviving point after the alternative explanation

Agreed. The issue is not whether operational action was permissible. The issue is that the later record should not retrospectively treat the operational allocation itself as proof that the original unresolved relationship had been clinically adjudicated.

## Evidential status

**Document-only.**

---

# 4. Stage Three — What management plan became durable?

## Question

What did the July 2017 treatment strategy commit the case to, and what did the record say would cause that strategy to change?

## Controlling propositions

- `P009` — Dopaminergic deferral was a deliberate management strategy
- `P010` — The plan expressly contemplated benefit, future necessity and possible regret
- `P011` — The surviving record does not identify one explicit reopening threshold for the deferral strategy
- `P012` — Diagnostic information was expressly valued partly by management impact
- `P014` — Prof Healy independently strengthened the PARK2/PRKN branch
- `P015` — PARK2/PRKN remained a specific live hypothesis in 2018

## Argument

The levodopa/dopamine-agonist deferral was not accidental or incidental. It was an articulated policy based on unusually young onset and concern about long-term treatment complications.

The same record expressly recognised the counterweight: dopamine replacement would probably become necessary; it might produce substantial benefit; and the team might later regret not having introduced it earlier.

That combination is important because the plan contains a recognised failure mode but the surviving record does not identify an equally explicit **escape condition** — a defined evidential, symptomatic or functional threshold that would require the strategy to be reconsidered.

A related pattern appears in the genetics reasoning. The record expressly asks what value a firm genetic diagnosis would have if it would not change management. By April 2018, the logic is stated more directly: there was “no great loss” in not knowing because the result would not particularly change current management.

Meanwhile, the PARK2/PRKN branch became increasingly specific. Prof Healy independently wrote that the patient probably had PARK II and proposed PARK II testing first. Galway later recorded PARK2/PRKN as the suspected cause.

The documentary concern is therefore not simply delay. It is the relationship between **management stability and information value**: the record shows diagnostic information being assessed partly by whether it would alter a plan already expected to remain substantially unchanged.

## Strongest alternative explanation

There may have been sound clinical reasons to defer both levodopa and genetic testing. Younger patients may face particular long-term treatment complications, and genetic confirmation may not change immediate symptomatic management.

## Surviving point after the alternative explanation

That explanation may justify the decisions clinically. It does not answer the documentary question of what evidence would reopen the strategy, or whether a management plan should also determine the value assigned to information capable of challenging its own assumptions.

## Evidential status

**Document-only for the existence and wording of the strategy.**  
**Expert evidence required** to determine whether the strategy was clinically appropriate.

# 5. Stage Four — What later evidence bore on the original unresolved relationship?

## Question

Did later records produce information capable of testing or qualifying the original 2017 foot–neurology uncertainty?

## Controlling propositions

- `P016` — Procyclidine was clinically material to the recorded motor state
- `P017` — Procyclidine withdrawal generated lower-limb/foot-specific information
- `P018` — A later foot-drop-like phenomenon was expressly classified as parkinsonian
- `P019` — The 2021 levodopa transition was tied to symptomatic deterioration and later dystonia/gait reasoning
- `P020` — By 2023 clinicians assigned OFF-state significance to leg pain and shuffling
- `P021` — The 2023 morning Stalevo change was explicitly response-generating
- `P022` — By 2025 dopaminergic treatment was expressly being used against the morning pain state
- `P023` — The cumulative treatment-state evidence bears on the original unresolved relationship

## Argument

No single later event is sufficient to prove one historical mechanism. The force lies in the accumulation.

The record documents meaningful improvement on Procyclidine and deterioration when it was withdrawn. The withdrawal episode includes a lower-limb/foot-specific signal: the rapid return of the outside-left-foot phenomenon alongside worsening tension and movement.

In 2020, a treating neurologist expressly classified a foot-throw resembling foot drop as parkinsonian after documenting normal ankle power.

In 2021, ordinary levodopa treatment finally entered the case in a context of worsening aches, pains and stiffness. By December 2021, “re-emerging dystonia and altered gait” were expressly used as reasons to increase levodopa exposure.

In 2023, severe foot/leg pain, reduced mobility and shuffling were documented in relation to medication timing, and a clinician assessed the leg pain and shuffling as OFF-related. A morning Stalevo substitution was then structured as a trial to see whether symptoms improved.

By 2025, Sinemet CR was being used in part to address the morning experience including pain, and a later clinician account described profound relief after escalation while still preserving mixed or peripheral mechanisms.

The proper proposition is therefore not that all foot pathology was neurological. It is that **the longitudinal medication-state record repeatedly generated evidence capable of bearing on the original unresolved lower-limb/foot relationship**.

## Strongest alternative explanation

The later phenomena may not all be identical. Structural pathology, neuropathic pain, altered loading, dystonia, OFF-state symptoms and dopaminergic pain modulation may have coexisted.

## Surviving point after the alternative explanation

That mixed-mechanism explanation is compatible with the case architecture. It actually strengthens the need for symptom-specific reconsideration rather than supporting a permanent binary partition.

## Evidential status

**Document-only for the existence of the later treatment-state evidence.**  
**Expert evidence required** for mechanism and causal allocation.

# 6. Stage Five — What happened to the original architecture when contrary or qualifying evidence accumulated?

## Question

Did later evidence merely change treatment locally, or did it cause the original 2017 case architecture to be reconstructed?

## Controlling propositions

- `P023` — The cumulative treatment-state evidence bears on the original unresolved relationship
- `P024` — Local updating is documented more clearly than global reconstruction
- `S006` — Treatment change does not itself prove global diagnostic reconsideration
- `S002` — The December 2021 / January 2022 dose reversal lacks a represented reasoning bridge
- `S003` — A narrow documented understanding should not be expanded into global agreement

## Argument

The later record is not static. Treatment changes repeatedly. Individual symptoms are reinterpreted. Dopaminergic coverage is altered. A foot phenomenon is later called parkinsonian. Leg pain and shuffling become OFF-related. Morning pain becomes a target of Sinemet CR.

Those are real updates.

But they are predominantly **local updates**: a prescription changes, a symptom is reclassified, a treatment trial is introduced, a dose is altered.

The presently represented record does not show an equally explicit document asking the global historical question again:

- What did the later medication-state evidence do to the original 2017 “Not clear” relationship?
- Did the Podiatry/Neurology partition still describe the case adequately?
- Did the later dystonia and OFF-related findings require the historical foot narrative to be revised?
- Did the PRKN branch alter the explanatory model?
- Which historical foot symptoms remained structural, which became neurologically mediated, and which remained unresolved?

That is the central documentary distinction:

> **The record permitted local updating while the surviving documentary architecture shows less evidence of global updating.**

This proposition is deliberately about the record, not the clinicians' private reasoning.

## Strongest alternative explanation

Global reconsideration may have occurred informally or may have been considered unnecessary because local treatment changes already reflected the clinically relevant updates.

## Surviving point after the alternative explanation

That is possible. The controlled proposition is only that such global reconstruction is not equivalently represented in the surviving documentary record.

## Evidential status

**Document-only as a record-architecture proposition.**  
Additional source material could qualify it.

# 7. Stage Six — What did later genetics do to the historical record?

## Question

Does the eventual PRKN result simply add a new diagnosis, or does it also illuminate the earlier documentary branch?

## Controlling propositions

- `P014` — Prof Healy independently strengthened the PARK2/PRKN branch
- `P015` — PARK2/PRKN remained a specific live hypothesis in 2018
- `P025` — The later etiological record does not reproduce a simple idiopathic-only history
- `S007` — Later genetic confirmation validates the earlier PRKN branch without determining every historical symptom mechanism

## Argument

The later PRKN result does not retrospectively prove the cause of every historical foot symptom.

Its documentary significance is different.

It confirms that the PARK2/PRKN branch was not an incidental hindsight construction. That branch existed contemporaneously in 2017–2018, was independently strengthened by Prof Healy, remained specifically suspected by Galway, and was eventually genetically confirmed.

Accordingly, later representations of the historical case as simply “idiopathic Parkinson's disease” do not reproduce the full earlier etiological record unless they are understood as syndrome-level shorthand.

## Strongest alternative explanation

“Idiopathic Parkinson's disease” may have been used loosely or phenomenologically rather than as a formal denial of genetic aetiology.

## Surviving point after the alternative explanation

That may reconcile the language. It does not erase the fact that the earlier documentary record contained a specific genetic hypothesis which later proved etiologically real.

## Evidential status

**Document-only for the historical existence of the PRKN branch and later confirmation.**  
Expert evidence required for symptom attribution.

# 8. The six propositions that carry the case

If the argument had to be reduced to six propositions, these should control:

1. **`P001` — One evolving unresolved presentation entered Neurology.**
2. **`P003` — The hospital itself left the foot–neurology relationship “Not clear”.**
3. **`P004` — Operational allocation occurred while that relationship remained unresolved.**
4. **`P009`–`P012` — A deliberate management strategy became durable while important explanatory questions remained open and no single reopening threshold is represented.**
5. **`P023` — Later pharmacological and neurological evidence repeatedly became capable of bearing on the original unresolved relationship.**
6. **`P024` — The surviving record documents local updating more clearly than global reconstruction of the original case.**

Everything else either proves, qualifies or protects those six.

---

# 9. What the documentary case can establish without expert evidence

The documents can establish:

- the pre-admission integrated referral state;
- the existence of genuine structural foot pathology;
- the neurological findings;
- the express “Not clear” relationship;
- the Podiatry pathway;
- the “as Parkinson's” Podiatry prioritisation;
- the deliberate dopaminergic-deferral strategy;
- the contemporaneous PARK2/PRKN branch;
- the timing of discharge, DaTSCAN and document finalisation;
- the recorded medication-state events;
- the later parkinsonian foot interpretation;
- the 2023 OFF-related assessment;
- the later pain-targeted dopaminergic strategy;
- the eventual PRKN result;
- the difference between what is expressly reconstructed globally and what is only changed locally.

---

# 10. What still requires expert evidence

Expert evidence is required before asserting:

- that a particular 2017 foot symptom was caused by dystonia or PRKN Parkinsonism;
- that Procyclidine response identifies the symptom generator;
- that levodopa should have been introduced at a particular earlier date;
- that the deferral strategy fell below a clinical standard;
- that the mixed structural/neurological model is medically the best causal explanation;
- that any delay caused a particular injury or deterioration;
- that later dopaminergic response retrospectively proves how every earlier symptom should have been classified.

---

# 11. What the case does not need

The documentary argument does not depend upon proving:

- that every foot symptom was neurological;
- that every structural abnormality was secondary to Parkinsonism;
- that Podiatry referral was inherently wrong;
- that levodopa had to be commenced during the July 2017 admission;
- that any clinician acted dishonestly or with a self-protective motive;
- that the bedside protest is necessary to establish the core clinical/documentary problem;
- that the 2026 PRKN result retrospectively resolves every symptom mechanism.

The reported patient protest, subjective pain experience and later patient recollection may be highly relevant to separate propositions about communication, objection, suffering and knowledge, but they are not load-bearing for the core documentary architecture.

---

# 12. Strongest institutional / innocent reconstruction

A fair opposing account would be:

> The patient had both genuine structural foot disease and a clear young-onset Parkinsonian syndrome. Neurology appropriately investigated and treated the Parkinsonism while Podiatry addressed independently visible structural disease. The exact relationship was uncertain, but uncertainty did not require paralysis of care. Levodopa was deliberately deferred because of the patient's age and anticipated long-term complications. Genetics was pursued cautiously because immediate management would not materially change. Later symptoms evolved, treatment options changed, and clinicians appropriately adapted medications locally. The later PRKN result clarified aetiology but did not prove that historical foot pain had been neurological. No single global reconstruction was necessary because the mixed-mechanism model already accommodated the longitudinal course.

This is a substantial alternative explanation and should be preserved.

---

# 13. Why the documentary case survives that reconstruction

The institutional account explains why individual decisions may have been reasonable.

It does not fully answer the documentary architecture question:

> **If the original relationship remained “Not clear”, and later records repeatedly supplied pharmacological, phenomenological and etiological information bearing on that same lower-limb/foot domain, where is the longitudinal reasoning that determines what changed, what remained separate, and why the original operational partition continued to provide an adequate governing history?**

That is the point at which the case should remain focused.

The strongest case is therefore not a retrospective demand for a single cause.

It is an audit of **how uncertainty was converted into durable representation, how later evidence was integrated, and whether the governing record remained proportionate to the evidence it accumulated.**

---

# 14. Final argument architecture

The full argument should proceed in this order:

### A. Entry
Establish the GP control record and the single evolving presentation.

### B. Hinge
Establish July neurological findings, structural pathology and “Not clear”.

### C. Conversion
Show the move from unresolved relationship to definite management and administrative architecture.

### D. Plan
Establish deliberate dopaminergic deferral, acknowledged future benefit, PRKN uncertainty and management-linked information value.

### E. Stress tests
Introduce Procyclidine withdrawal, later parkinsonian foot interpretation, levodopa/dystonia reasoning, OFF-related pain and 2025 dopamine-linked pain management.

### F. Etiological confirmation
Show that the contemporaneous PRKN branch was later confirmed.

### G. Reconstruction question
Ask whether the original case architecture was ever equivalently rebuilt in light of those later developments.

### H. Boundary
Preserve structural pathology, mixed mechanisms, legitimate Podiatry involvement and the need for expert evidence on causation and clinical standard.

---

# 15. Controlling formulation

> **The central documentary issue is not whether every foot symptom was neurological. It is what evidence justified converting an unresolved relationship into a durable operational separation, and where the surviving record shows that later pharmacological, neurological and etiological evidence was permitted to reconstruct that original relationship rather than merely alter treatment around it.**
