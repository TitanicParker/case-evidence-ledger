> **Control rule:** A proposition synthesises identified documentary facts. It is not itself a raw fact and does not determine negligence, clinical standard, exclusive causation, motive, dishonesty or legal effect.

## Governing proposition

The contemporaneous documentary record begins with one unresolved clinical presentation and later shows a durable operational separation within that presentation before the relationship between its parts had been resolved. Subsequent documentary evidence repeatedly supplied information capable of bearing on that unresolved relationship, but the surviving record shows local treatment and interpretive updating more clearly than an equivalent reconstruction of the original case.

# III. Evidential control rules

## C001 — Original source controls over later reproduction

A later bundle or complaint response repeating an older record does not create independent corroboration merely through repetition.

## C002 — Patient report is not automatically objective finding

A contemporaneously recorded patient report is evidence of the report and may be clinically important, but it is not silently converted into an independently observed fact.

## C003 — Administrative classification is not identical to etiological conclusion

Administrative use of “Parkinson's disease” can coexist with unresolved subtype or genetic aetiology.

## C004 — Medication response is not exclusive causation proof

A pharmacological response can bear materially on phenotype and classification without identifying the sole generator of every symptom.

## C005 — Documentary absence is not historical non-occurrence

“The surviving record does not show X” must not be converted into “X never happened” without additional evidence.

# VI. Governing boundary

The documentary case does **not** require proof that every foot symptom was neurological, that Podiatry was inappropriate, that levodopa had to be started in July 2017, that any clinician acted dishonestly, or that later PRKN confirmation retrospectively determines the mechanism of every historical symptom.

Its strongest form is narrower:

> **The record converted unresolved clinical uncertainty into durable operational architecture, and later evidence repeatedly became relevant to the unresolved relationship without the surviving record showing an equivalent global reconstruction of that architecture.**
