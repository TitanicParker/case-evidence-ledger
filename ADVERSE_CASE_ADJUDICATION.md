# Case Evidence Ledger — Adverse Case Adjudication

**Status:** Controlled adjudication of independent defence audit  
**Primary basis:** `DEFENCE_ADVERSE_CASE_AUDIT.md`, `PROPOSITION_REGISTER.md`, `CASE_ARGUMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`  
**Purpose:** Determine which defence answers defeat, qualify, coexist with, or require expert adjudication of the controlled claimant propositions.

> **Control rule:** This document does not rebut the defence for rhetorical effect. It preserves any defence point that genuinely contracts the claimant case and distinguishes documentary propositions from clinical-standard, causation, governance and legal conclusions.

---

# 1. Adjudication classes

Each defence answer is assigned one of four outcomes.

### A — Defeats / requires amendment
The defence exposes a genuine defect in the proposition as presently framed. The proposition should be contracted, rewritten or removed.

### B — Material qualification
The proposition survives, but the defence identifies a qualification that should travel with it permanently.

### C — Coexists but does not answer
The defence explanation may be entirely true and important, but it does not displace the documentary proposition.

### D — Requires expert adjudication
The documentary proposition survives, but neither side can legitimately convert it into a clinical-standard, causation, mechanism or duty conclusion without expert evidence.

A proposition may carry more than one class where the defence both qualifies the documentary point and pushes its clinical significance into expert territory.

---

# 2. Executive adjudication

The independent defence audit does **not** identify any core documentary proposition that must be abandoned.

Its own final verdict is that the **claimant documentary case materially survives**.

The defence succeeds principally in four ways:

1. It blocks any attempt to convert Podiatry involvement into proof of wrongdoing.
2. It blocks any attempt to convert medication response into proof of one exclusive historical mechanism.
3. It blocks any attempt to convert later PRKN confirmation into proof that earlier testing or treatment was necessarily unreasonable.
4. It blocks any attempt to convert absence of a global reconstruction document into proof that no reconsideration occurred.

Those are real and important limitations.

But the defence does **not** answer the core documentary sequence:

- the presentation entered Neurology in an integrated unresolved state;
- the hospital itself recorded the key relationship as “Not clear”;
- definite operational allocation nevertheless followed;
- later pharmacological, neurological and genetic information repeatedly became relevant to the original uncertainty;
- the represented record contains clearer local updates than a later explicit global reconstruction.

The defence's strongest answers therefore mostly fall into **B, C and D**, not A.

# 6. The five defence points that genuinely contract the claimant case

## 1. Podiatry was not inherently improper

The claimant case must never be framed as:

> “Podiatry should not have been involved.”

The sustainable question is what role the Podiatry pathway came to play in the governing representation of the case.

## 2. “Not clear” did not require clinical paralysis

The record could rationally support simultaneous Neurology and Podiatry care despite unresolved mechanism.

## 3. Medication response is not exclusive mechanism proof

Procyclidine, levodopa, Stalevo and Sinemet CR evidence can support relevance and reopening, but not sole causation without expert evidence.

## 4. PRKN confirmation cannot be used as hindsight proof of earlier breach

Its strongest use is to establish that the earlier PRKN branch was genuinely live and ultimately correct.

## 5. Missing synthesis does not prove missing thought

P024 must remain a proposition about the surviving record, not about unrecorded cognition.

# 9. The strongest surviving documentary case after hostile audit

After giving the defence all legitimate qualifications, the case can be stated more securely as follows:

> **The patient entered Neurology through one evolving presentation in which feet, gait, movement initiation, tone, dexterity and functional decline were clinically connected enough to require neurological assessment. During the July 2017 admission, the hospital documented both genuine structural foot disease and severe neurological lower-limb abnormalities while expressly leaving their relationship “Not clear”. A definite operational allocation nevertheless followed, with Parkinsonism under Neurology and the feet routed through Podiatry. That allocation did not itself prove etiological separateness and may have been entirely reasonable as parallel care. The subsequent record then accumulated medication-state, phenomenological and genetic information capable of bearing on the original unresolved relationship. The surviving record clearly shows local treatment and interpretive updating, but does not show an equally explicit longitudinal reconstruction stating what those later developments did to the original 2017 foot–neurology relationship. That is a documentary gap, not by itself proof of negligence, causation or absence of clinical thought.**

This formulation survives the defence audit.

# 13. What should now change in the case architecture

The existing `CASE_ARGUMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md` remains fundamentally sound.

Three refinements should govern its future use:

### First
Replace any rhetorical use of **“foreclosure”** with **“operational allocation,” “durable pathway separation,”** or **“governing partition”** unless the proposition being advanced is specifically about later representational foreclosure.

### Second
When discussing P024, always state both halves:

> local updating is clearly documented;

and

> an equally explicit global reconstruction is not represented.

Do not say “there was no reconsideration.”

### Third
Treat the adverse case not as an external rebuttal but as a permanent part of the proof architecture. Every final submission should be capable of acknowledging the strongest defence explanation before stating what remains unresolved.
