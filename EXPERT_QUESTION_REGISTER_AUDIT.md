# Expert Question Register — Coverage and Neutrality Audit

**Status:** Pre-integration audit of `EXPERT_QUESTION_REGISTER.md`  
**Scope:** All 22 Evidential Tensions, all 25 core Propositions, Defence Adverse Case Audit and Adverse Case Adjudication.

## Audit result

The initial eight-question register was conceptually coherent and did not materially duplicate questions, but it had one substantive expert-gated coverage gap: `P013` (stated movement-disorders limitation coexisting with durable operative authority) was not directly converted into an expert question. That gap is now closed by `EQ009` in the controlled register.

The remaining apparently uncovered tensions should not become standalone expert questions:

- `T018` — physical discharge / DaTSCAN / finalisation chronology: documentary chronology, not expert opinion.
- `T019` — hospital activity / GP receipt chronology: source and transmission chronology, not expert opinion.
- `T020` — narrow contemporaneous understanding / later shared-understanding representation: representation-scope issue, not primarily a movement-disorders question.
- `T021` — December 2021 increase / January 2022 reduction without represented bridge: missing-source issue first; its clinical significance is already capable of being considered within EQ005 if the missing material is obtained.

`P006`–`P008` are documentary controls rather than independent expert-question generators. Their clinical implications are captured by EQ006, EQ009 and the temporal controls. `P012` is properly captured by EQ006 and EQ009. `P014`–`P015` are properly captured by EQ006 and EQ009.

## Neutrality audit

No controlled question requires a claimant-favourable answer.

The strongest controls are preserved:

- EQ002 permits the expert to conclude that parallel Neurology/Podiatry management was reasonable and required no special synthesis.
- EQ003 permits the expert to conclude that the dopaminergic deferral strategy and duration were reasonable.
- EQ004 permits the expert to treat Procyclidine evidence as nonspecific or insufficient.
- EQ005 expressly permits limited or indeterminate longitudinal continuity.
- EQ006 permits the expert to conclude that PRKN confirmation had little bearing on earlier management.
- EQ007 permits the expert to conclude that local updating was clinically sufficient and no distinct global reconstruction was required.
- EQ009 permits the expert to conclude that existing specialist input was adequate and no further formal referral was required.
- EQ008 permits no causative harm or insufficient evidence.

The register therefore exposes both claimant-supporting and defence-supporting expert outcomes.

## Duplication audit

The principal overlaps are justified rather than duplicative:

- EQ001 defines the 2017 phenotype; EQ002 asks whether the operational response to that phenotype was adequate.
- EQ004 isolates the Procyclidine episode; EQ005 asks whether the broader longitudinal phenomena are clinically continuous.
- EQ005 establishes longitudinal clinical relevance; EQ007 asks whether that relevance required broader reconsideration.
- EQ009 addresses adequacy of specialist escalation, not the substantive treatment-timing question in EQ003.
- EQ008 is deliberately downstream and conditional; it must not absorb standard-of-care or mechanism questions from earlier EQs.

No question should presently be collapsed.

## Coverage conclusion

With EQ009 integrated, every expert-dependent cluster in the 25 core Propositions is mapped to an expert question. The four tensions not used as independent question triggers (`T018`–`T021`) are deliberately retained as documentary/source controls rather than being artificially medicalised.

The controlled set is therefore **EQ001–EQ009**, with EQ008 remaining last in substantive sequence because causation must remain downstream of all liability/mechanism questions even though identifier numbering is retained for stability.
