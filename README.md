# Case Evidence Ledger

## Governing documentary proposition

> **The contemporaneous documentary record begins with one unresolved clinical presentation and later shows that the institution created and maintained a durable separation within that presentation before the relationship between its parts had been resolved. Subsequent documentary evidence repeatedly supplied reasons to revisit that separation, but the surviving record does not show an equivalent reconstruction of the original case.**

This repository is designed as a clean evidential ledger rather than a migration of the older `for-counsel` architecture. The documentary record comes first; interpretation follows from it.

## The case in documentary terms

The GP record begins with bilateral foot pain and abnormal gait, then records impaired movement initiation, the combined question of the patient's **“gait, feet and 'muscle tension' issues”**, increased tone in both legs, worsening dexterity and failure of work function. By 29 June 2017 the presentation had escalated to direct hospital neurological assessment.

During the July admission, Neurology identified severe young-onset Parkinsonism, genuine structural foot pathology and a striking lower-limb gait with dystonic-looking features. The surviving hospital record did not resolve the relationship between those domains. Its recorded state was **“Not clear.”**

Nevertheless, a durable operative architecture emerged: Parkinsonism remained with Neurology; the feet were routed through Podiatry; ordinary dopaminergic therapy was deliberately deferred; and Parkinson's disease became an administrative category. The 6 July GP record is important because Podiatry itself was being prioritised **“as Parkinson's”**, so the referral pathway cannot retrospectively establish that the feet had already been shown to be neurologically separate.

The later documentary record repeatedly adds information capable of bearing on that unresolved relationship: neurological medication changes the lower-limb/foot phenotype; PARK2/PRKN becomes a specialist-supported diagnostic branch; a later foot movement is expressly described as parkinsonian; OFF/dystonia reasoning enters the foot-pain record; dopamine timing is eventually used deliberately against severe morning foot pain; and the PRKN branch is later genetically confirmed.

The central question is therefore not whether every historical foot symptom was neurological. It is:

> **What evidence justified converting uncertainty about the relationship into a durable management separation, and where does the surviving record show that later neurological, pharmacological and etiological evidence was permitted to reconstruct the original case rather than merely alter local treatment?**

## Governing distinction

> **What the organism did versus what the record said the organism meant.**

## Evidential discipline

This repository does not require or assume that every corn, callus, toe deformity or pain episode had one neurological cause; that Podiatry was inherently inappropriate; that levodopa had to be commenced in July 2017; or that negligence, dishonesty, concealment or motive is established.

The evidence layer should remain cleaner than the argument layer. Exact source reproductions preserve source fidelity; EUIDs provide unique addressability; duplicate source-export occurrences should not acquire the appearance of independent corroboration; and embedded copies of already-controlled documents should create new evidence units only where they independently establish receipt, transmission, coding, reliance or other provenance facts.

## Working order

**Source → EUID → tested proposition → reader-facing chronology → argument.**

The consolidated corpus controls over this README and over the timeline wherever wording or source status differs.
